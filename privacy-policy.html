JDevsAppStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers JDevsAppStudio's internal developer tools, such as JDevs Drive Sync, a private utility the studio uses to copy its own files to and from its own Google Drive. These tools are not offered to the public. Each of our Android apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its page on this site.

What JDevs Drive Sync accesses

When the studio's own Google account authorizes it, the tool can list, read, upload and download files in that account's Google Drive. It does this only on the studio's own computer, on request, to move the studio's own files (for example app builds, screenshots and backups).

What we do not do

  • We do not access anyone else's Google account. The tool is only authorized for the studio's own account.
  • Files and Google account data are never sent to any server run by us. Transfers go directly between the computer and Google.
  • We do not sell, share or use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.
  • Access tokens stay on the studio's computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions about privacy? Email jdevsappstudio@gmail.com.
