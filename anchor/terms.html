Anchor Plus — the subscription

Anchor Plus is a yearly auto-renewing subscription processed through Google Play Billing. It unlocks additional themes, focus scenes, soundscapes, builds, insights and history; it never changes how quickly you earn anything, and the core timer, tasks and graph are never behind it.

  • Free trial. First-time subscribers may be offered a free trial. If you do nothing, the subscription starts when the trial ends and Google Play charges the price shown to you at purchase. The price is shown in your local currency by Google Play before you confirm. You can cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged. A free trial is offered once per user.
  • Renewal. The subscription renews each year at the then-current price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancelling. Manage or cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; Anchor's "Manage subscription" opens that page. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Anchor Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Refunds. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policies, not directly by us.
  • If Plus ends. Plus features lock. Nothing is deleted: a Plus build leaves the yard but keeps every plank it earned, and resubscribing restores everything exactly.
  • Restoring. "Restore purchases" (powered by RevenueCat) re-grants Anchor Plus on any device signed into the Google account that bought it.
