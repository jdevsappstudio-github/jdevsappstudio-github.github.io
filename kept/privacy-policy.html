Kept privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Kept saves screenshots, links and shared posts on your phone and makes them searchable. This page says what Kept keeps, what leaves your phone, and when. Kept is made by JDevsAppStudio ("we").

In short
  • Your saves stay on your phone. Kept has no account and no server of its own.
  • Saves are sent to Google's Gemini models only when Kept reads them in the cloud, when you use Ask, or when you tap Fill in with cloud. Every request is logged in the app.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your saves: the copied images, thumbnails, links, shared text, and the cards Kept makes from them (titles, tags, items, notes).
  • Your collections, shelves, recent searches, the Ask chat, and the "What was sent" log.
  • Your settings.

Nothing above is uploaded or backed up by Kept.

What leaves your phone, and when

Kept uses Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic. Data goes to Google only in these cases:

WhenWhat is sent
A save is read in the cloud. This happens only with Cloud help on. On a phone without on-device AI (Gemini Nano), Cloud help covers reading saves, and Kept asks you first. The save's image (scaled down) and any text or link that came with it
You ask a question in Ask (Cloud help on) Your question and the text of the saves that best match it. Never images.
You tap Fill in with cloud on a link The link, so Gemini can read that page

On a phone with Gemini Nano, saves are read on the phone and nothing is sent.

Every request is listed in Settings › Privacy and AI › What was sent, with the exact text, for 30 days.

Kept uses the paid tier of the Gemini Developer API. Under its terms, Google processes what is sent only to answer the request and does not use it to improve its products. See the Gemini API terms.

Other Google services

  • Firebase App Check with Play Integrity confirms that requests come from the real Kept app.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches settings such as which model to use and daily limits. It sends your country, language, time zone and Android version, as every Remote Config app does.

Both use a Firebase installation ID, a random ID for this install of Kept. Neither receives your saves. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

What Kept does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • No access to your photos beyond the images you pick or share with Kept.
  • No account, so no email, name or phone number.

Notifications

Kept posts one optional notification about saves waiting to be read. Turn it off in Settings › Reading updates.

Deleting your data

Settings › Privacy and AI › Delete everything removes every save, image, collection, the Ask chat and the sent log from your phone. Uninstalling Kept removes everything as well. We hold no copy of your saves, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Kept is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top are updated. The same text is kept in the app's public repository, with its history.
