Clock OutOffice Survivor

Dodge pings, beat your bosses and climb the ladder. The door opens at 5 PM.

You're a new developer in an office nobody has ever left. Chat pings swarm you, P0 tickets rush you, and the meeting invites keep coming. Keep moving and make it to the door.

Get it on Google Play

Free to play. Optional ads, and one optional Pro purchase.

One thumb

Move. Your shots fire themselves.

Drag to dodge and auto-fire handles the rest. Every stage is a short, crowded shift: finish your tasks, catch your coworkers so they work beside you, build desks to stop the pings, and pick up office powers like Hotfix and Rubber Duck.

14 bosses

Survive your bosses

Fourteen bosses stand between you and the exit, each with their own move. Dodge the Scrum Master's standup ring, walk out of a Quick 1:1 with your Eng Manager, and find a safe spot when the CEO announces Layoffs.

70 stages

Climb the career ladder

Start as an Intern and work your way up through 7 job titles and 70 stages. Earn stars, get promoted, and find out what's waiting at the very top.

Your desk

Make the desk your own

Your workstation is your home screen. Unlock new gear, level it up for better stats and pay, and dress up in skins from Hoodie Dev to Golden Parachute.

Overtime, if you dare

Finish enough tasks and the door opens early. Leave now, or stay until 5:00 for overtime pay.

Play your way

Easy, Medium and Hard. A daily gift, and no energy timer.

Fair by design

Ads only when you choose one, to revive or double your pay. Pro removes them, adds looks only, and never adds power.

Ready to clock out?

If you've ever been told a meeting would only take five minutes, this one is for you.
